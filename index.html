Privacy Policy

XMinder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-22

Short summary: XMinder requests notification access only to detect relevant notifications and provide alerts inside the app. Notification data is processed entirely locally on your device and is not intentionally uploaded, sold, or shared with our servers. If third-party services such as advertising SDKs are enabled, they may collect data according to their own policies, but they do not receive notification contents for advertising purposes.

1. Overview

XMinder is a notification-based productivity and alert app. Its core function depends on Android’s notification access feature so it can detect selected notifications and trigger in-app behavior for the user.

We aim to minimize data collection. In normal use, XMinder processes notification data locally on the device and does not transmit notification contents to our servers.

2. Information We Process

a) Notification Data

When notification access is enabled, XMinder can view notification events delivered by the Android operating system. This may include:

  • Notification title
  • Notification text or message content
  • App package name that generated the notification
  • Notification timestamp and basic metadata

This information is processed locally to power the app’s alerting and monitoring features.

b) User-entered App Data

XMinder may store information you manually enter into the app, such as:

  • Profiles or usernames you choose to monitor
  • App preferences
  • Sound or vibration settings
  • Ringtone selections
  • Privacy-policy acceptance state
  • Onboarding or permission prompt state

c) Technical and Device Information

XMinder and its third-party services may process limited technical information such as:

  • Android version
  • Device model
  • App version
  • Crash or diagnostic information
  • Advertising identifiers if advertising is enabled

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Detect notifications and trigger app alerts
  • Show relevant content inside XMinder
  • Store preferences and app configuration
  • Improve app stability and compatibility
  • Deliver advertising if enabled
  • Comply with legal requirements

We do not use notification contents to build marketing profiles or to sell personal data.

4. Local Processing of Notification Data

Notification access is used to support XMinder’s core functionality. Notification data is processed entirely on-device for identifying relevant alerts and applying user-selected app behavior.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, XMinder does not intentionally upload notification contents to remote servers.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell notification contents.

Information may be shared only in limited situations:

  • With service providers such as advertising or analytics SDKs
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect rights, users, or platform security

6. Third-party Services

XMinder may include third-party services such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and device settings.

XMinder does not permit third-party services to access notification contents for advertising purposes.

7. Permissions We Request

  • Notification Access / Notification Listener Service
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS
  • INTERNET
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS

We only request permissions necessary for app functionality.

8. Storage and Retention

XMinder stores app configuration and user preferences locally on your device.

If you uninstall the app, locally stored app data is generally removed according to Android behavior.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to reduce unauthorized access or disclosure of data.

10. Children’s Privacy

XMinder is not intended for children under the legally required age for data consent in applicable jurisdictions.

11. Your Choices and Controls

  • Enable or disable notification access
  • Grant or revoke notification permissions
  • Adjust battery optimization settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data

Disabling notification access may disable some app features.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect feature, legal, or platform changes.
